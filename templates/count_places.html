Jaipur

Description

Jaipur, the capital of Rajasthan, is known as the Pink City due to the dominant color scheme of its buildings. It was founded in 1727 by Maharaja Sawai Jai Singh II and is a major tourist destination in India, forming a part of the Golden Triangle.

Best Season

See

The best season to visit Jaipur is from October to March when the weather is extremely pleasant and ideal for sightseeing monuments and enjoying local heritage festivals.
